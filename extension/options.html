The address is set in one place: externally_connectable → matches in this extension's manifest.json. Edge itself refuses messages from any other site. After editing the file, open edge://extensions and press Reload on this extension.

Why these permissions

  • All sites (<all_urls>) - Edge only allows taking a screenshot of a page when the extension has this permission (a narrower grant was tested and is refused). It is also used to read a page's address, title and load status. The extension never reads page text, form values, passwords or cookies.
  • scripting - to check that a page really loaded, to scroll for full-page capture, and to confirm the zoom level.
  • storage / unlimitedStorage - to keep your settings, URL list and screenshots on this computer only.

Your data

Everything is stored locally in Edge. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Passwords, MFA codes, cookies and session tokens are never stored.

Stored right now: – screenshot(s). To see exactly what is stored, open edge://extensions → this extension → Inspect views: service worker → Application tab.

Deleting data does not sign you out of any website. To remove the extension itself, open edge://extensions and choose Remove.
